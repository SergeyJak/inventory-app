## How to order a Yandex Station from Latvia

1. Choose a model and colour from the [HeySmart catalogue](/en). Availability and prices can change, so we confirm the stock before arranging an order.
2. Send us your destination country, city, postcode, preferred model and quantity. We request the complete delivery address when preparing the shipment.
3. We check whether delivery is available and quote the shipping charge, expected timeframe and total price for the selected service.
4. Once the price and delivery terms are agreed, we confirm the order. Tracking details can be shared after the courier accepts the parcel.

**International shipping is not automatically included in the listed speaker price.** A shipping quote depends on destination, parcel size, weight, carrier service and acceptance conditions. Another customer's quote is not a reliable estimate for your shipment.
