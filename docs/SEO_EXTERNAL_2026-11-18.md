# HeySmart: Latvia classifieds and earned-mention campaign

Researched: 2026-11-18. Target audience: people in Riga and Latvia searching for Alice/Yandex Station smart speakers, **Russian first, English second**. Status: **prepared, NOT published**.

This is primarily a local buyer-acquisition campaign, not a promise of followed backlinks or SEO positions. A listing may be indexed, blocked, nofollowed, or removed; check the live URL after publication and measure real leads.

## Publication priority and verified rules

1. **SS.LV** — https://www.ss.lv/ru/electronics/other/smart-house-arduino-raspberry-pi/sell/ . Existing Yandex Mini 3/Pro competitors appear here. One specific item per ad, true final price and real photo of that precise item. Do **not** put phone, email, URL, logos or website text in the description or images: SS.LV section 5.6 prohibits it; use dedicated fields if available. An external website URL, if supported, must link **directly to that product**, never just the homepage (5.22). Text for different product ads must be distinct (5.31). Price and duration depend on the section; posting may cost money. Official terms: https://www.ss.lv/ru/rules/ . **Only publish after checking exact stock, final price, picture and cost; no unapproved payment.**
2. **MM.LV** — https://mm.lv/item/new . Supports Russian, English, Latvian text tabs, Electronics category and a website field. The form explicitly separates individual/noncommercial and business accounts: use the **truthful** seller classification; do not mark regular commercial inventory as noncommercial to evade terms. Publication requires a user account/verification. Source: https://mm.lv/item/new .
3. **AdSnap** — https://adsnap.lv/en/create-listing ; Electronics → Smart Home or Audio Equipment. Platform advertises free ads and up to 30 photos. Requires account access. Source: https://adsnap.lv/en/electronics .
4. **Dalder** — https://www.dalder.lv/en ; Electronics → Riga, free classified posts (business-store basket transactions may incur a fee). Requires registration. Source: https://www.dalder.lv/en .
5. **Listio** — https://listio.lv/ ; Electronics. Listings are free, but site rules forbid generic brand advertising without a concrete real product, detailed terms and an actual offer. Requires account access. Sources: https://listio.lv/ and https://listio.lv/terms .

Do not create a fictitious company, showroom, public business address or Google Maps location. Google Business Profile eligibility is not automatic for an online-only/appointment-only seller: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177 . Generic business directories that require a public company address are **not** a priority.

## Publishing checklist

For **each** ad, select **one exact stocked model and colour** and verify stock and price in HeySmart inventory immediately before publishing. Mini 3 violet and Mini 3 Pro blue had previously had storefront visibility issues: check availability carefully. Use an unedited clear **photo of the actual selling item** (particularly essential on SS.LV), not a downloaded promotional/rendered catalog photo. Confirm factual item condition, packaging and optional accessories; do not suggest official Yandex reseller or guaranteed functionality across regions. Delivery costs should be transparently listed if mandatory.

Provide an individual direct product page as website URL **only when the platform permits it in a website field**. For SS.LV never put the URL in the description itself. If a platform permits tracking query strings, use the UTM URL below; otherwise use the clean canonical URL.
